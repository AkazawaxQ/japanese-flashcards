# Data attribution

The JLPT Kanji data used by this app comes from OpenJLPT (evanclan/OpenJLPT), built from community JLPT level lists, JMdict and KANJIDIC2. The dataset is distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0.

Important: the Japan Foundation / JEES does not publish an official current JLPT kanji list. N5–N1 levels in this app are therefore study-oriented community approximations, not a guarantee of which characters will appear on a specific exam.
